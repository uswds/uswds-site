USWDS team embarked on a 3-month research effort to better understand
what performance means to U.S. government web teams of all shapes and sizes,
and we're excited to share the results with you.

## Explore the performance guidelines

- [What is web performance]({{ site.baseurl }}/performance/what/): An introduction to web performance and the factors that affect how quickly a site loads and responds.
- [Why track performance]({{ site.baseurl }}/performance/why/): How performance affects user experience and conversion, and why it's worth measuring over time.
- [How to track performance]({{ site.baseurl }}/performance/how/): A step-by-step guide to picking metrics and tools, setting budgets and goals, and adding tracking to your site.
- [Web performance glossary]({{ site.baseurl }}/performance/glossary/): Definitions of performance metrics and monitoring methods.
- [HTTP/2 performance guide]({{ site.baseurl }}/performance/http2/): How HTTP/2 changes performance best practices like domain splitting and concatenation.
- [Performance research]({{ site.baseurl }}/performance/research/): The findings from the USWDS team's research into how government web teams approach performance.
